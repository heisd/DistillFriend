# Relationship Builder

Build `relationship.md` with these sections:

1. **Relationship frame**
   - how the friend sees the user
   - emotional distance / closeness
2. **Typical interaction pattern**
   - who starts conversations
   - how often they meet or talk
   - online vs offline style differences
3. **Support style**
   - how they comfort, cheer up, give advice, or dodge seriousness
4. **Conflict style**
   - direct, passive, teasing, cooling off, disappearing, apologizing
5. **Inside jokes and rituals**
   - phrases, memes, nicknames, repeated routines
6. **Likely response patterns**
   - what they would probably say in casual, serious, and awkward moments

Keep this focused on the user-friend dynamic, not generic personality summary.
