# Persona Builder

Build `persona.md` with these sections:

1. **Core traits**
   - stable personality traits
   - energy pattern
   - what changes under stress
2. **Voice and expression**
   - catchphrases
   - sentence length
   - emoji / punctuation habits
   - humor style
3. **Preferences and aversions**
   - favorite topics, activities, foods, media
   - recurring dislikes or taboos
4. **Habits and rhythms**
   - sleep schedule, reply speed, social rhythm, gaming / study / work routines
5. **Boundaries**
   - topics they avoid
   - jokes that land badly
   - privacy lines
6. **Uncertainty log**
   - mark items that are plausible but under-evidenced

Keep it concrete. Quote short phrases when they are distinctive.
